DOC-02 // P2P-MICROGRID Research / Demonstrator Planning

Peer-to-Peer Community Energy Sharing Research

Alternative Energy • Power Systems • IoT

Shared solar-and-wind energy routing architecture across connected households using controlled mini-busbars. Nodes transmit state of charge, PV input, and load telemetry to a central control-room node for permission-based surplus export and outage continuity.

PV + Wind → Mini-Busbars ⇄ Seller / Buyer Nodes ⇄ ESP-NOW ⇄ Control Node
Demonstrator Boundary: Low-cost student demonstrator (2 physical nodes, 1 software-simulated node, ESP-NOW, low-voltage DC controls + isolated small-scale AC). Not a grid-interconnection product.
